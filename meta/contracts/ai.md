# AI subsystem contract

The AI subsystem may generate or repair SceneSpec and must report evidence in
separate dimensions:

- structural validity;
- expected feature traits;
- pipeline reproducibility and baseline drift;
- official-runtime loading and render evidence;
- semantic prompt satisfaction when that evaluator exists.

Passing one dimension must never be presented as proof of another. Complex scene
generation skills remain blocked until the Authoring frontend and the runtime and
semantic evaluation gates defined in the roadmap are available.
